# Facet Question Templates

Standard clarification phrasing per missing facet:

## Unit Dimension
- **Question:** Which unit of measure applies to this lab measurement?
- **Allowed Options:** Mass concentration (e.g. standard blood units) or Fraction (e.g. percentage).

## Calcium Fraction
- **Question:** Is this a Total Calcium or an Ionized Calcium measurement?
- **Allowed Options:** `total` or `ionized`.

## Troponin Assay
- **Question:** Please specify whether this assay is cardiac Troponin I or high-sensitivity Troponin T.
- **Allowed Options:** `cTnI` or `hs-cTnT`.
